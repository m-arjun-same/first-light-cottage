Foxglove & Ember

[FOXGLOVE & EMBER] proper noun • a little house in Foxglove Hollow where morning starts slow

A small cottage where you come in “just for one cup”, before staying for breakfast, then another cup, then finally settling into a sunny nook much longer than planned. A place built to slow you down, known to cause cries of “stay awhile” and mornings that rarely end at the time announced.
